9K Roll Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026
9K Roll is designed to work offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. Saved Quick setups remain in the app's private local storage on your device.

This Privacy Policy applies to 9K Roll - Dice, Wheel & Picker (“9K Roll”).

1. Data collection and sharing

9K Roll does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data or sensitive user data.

The app does not use:

  • advertising SDKs;
  • analytics or tracking SDKs;
  • user accounts or authentication services;
  • cloud storage or sync;
  • remote servers for app functionality; or
  • social SDKs.

9K Roll does not request Android's INTERNET permission.

2. Local data

Some features allow you to create and save data locally on your device. This can include:

  • Quick names;
  • saved dice configurations;
  • saved number ranges; and
  • saved Wheel option lists.

This information is stored in the app's private local storage and is used only to provide the app's local functionality. 9K Roll does not transmit this information to 9K Studio or to third parties.

If the app detects a damaged Quick library, it may keep a local recovery copy inside the app's private storage to reduce the risk of destructive data loss. This recovery copy is not transmitted off the device.

3. Random results

Dice rolls, coin flips, random numbers, Wheel selections, and Fingers selections are generated locally on the device.

9K Roll does not send these results to a server or use them for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking.

4. Device backup and transfer

Android operating-system backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for 9K Roll.

App-private data is intended to remain on the device where it was created unless you manually move or otherwise copy data outside the app through operating-system or user-controlled mechanisms.

5. Data security

Locally stored app data is kept in Android app-private storage and is protected by the Android application sandbox.

Because 9K Roll does not request Internet access and does not operate a backend service, the app does not transmit its local user data to 9K Studio.

6. Data retention and deletion

Saved Quick data remains on the device until you delete it in the app, clear 9K Roll's app data through Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes the app's private local data from that device, subject to the behavior of the Android operating system.

9K Roll does not maintain a remote copy of this data.

7. Third-party services

9K Roll does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, authentication, or cloud-storage SDKs.

Android system and AndroidX components used to run the app operate as part of the local Android application environment.

8. Children's privacy

9K Roll does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the app does not collect personal information.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If 9K Roll's data practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
